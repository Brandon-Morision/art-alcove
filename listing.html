Painted during a residency on the Kenyan coast, Golden Hour Study captures the brief daily moment when the horizon dissolves into warm light — what Elena calls "the hour when everything becomes its own painting."

Built up in multiple glazed layers over a textured ground, the canvas rewards close viewing: the surface is alive with subtle brushwork and tonal variations that photographs cannot fully convey. A Certificate of Authenticity signed by the artist is included.
