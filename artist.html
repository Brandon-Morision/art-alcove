Biography

The Artist & Her Practice

Born in Seville in 1988, Elena Vasquez studied Fine Art at the Real Academia de Bellas Artes de San Fernando in Madrid before completing a postgraduate residency at the British School at Rome. It was in Rome that she first developed her signature approach to painting light — an approach that would define her practice for the next decade.

"I am not painting things. I am painting the air between things, the way light behaves when it touches a surface and leaves it."

Her work is held in private collections across Europe, the Americas, and Asia. She has exhibited in major group shows at the Reina Sofía, the Saatchi Gallery, and the Frieze Art Fair, and has been the subject of three solo exhibitions in Madrid, London, and New York.

Vasquez works primarily in oil on canvas, though her practice extends to monotype printmaking and occasional large-format charcoal works. Her studio in the Lavapiés district of Madrid doubles as a teaching space where she runs intensive workshops twice a year.

She is represented by Galería Mara Castellanos (Madrid) and Bright Street Gallery (London). Works on Art Alcove are sold directly by the artist, without gallery markup.
