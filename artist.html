About Elena

Elena Vasquez was born in Mombasa in 1988 and grew up between Kenya and Portugal, an upbringing that gave her a dual sensitivity to light — the hard brilliance of the equatorial sun and the soft, melancholic quality of Atlantic afternoons.

She studied Fine Art at the University of Nairobi before completing a postgraduate residency at the Fondazione Pistoletto in Biella, Italy. It was there that she developed her signature technique: building up oil glazes over textured resin grounds, a method that gives her paintings their characteristic luminous depth.

"Painting is how I process the world. Each canvas is an attempt to hold light still long enough to understand it."

Since returning to Nairobi in 2016, Elena has held twelve solo exhibitions across Kenya, the UK and the United States. Her work is held in private collections in 28 countries, and she has been shortlisted for the Mbokazi Prize for Contemporary African Painting twice.

She works from a studio in Westlands and teaches advanced oil technique workshops three times a year.
